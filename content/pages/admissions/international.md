## Welcome to India, Welcome to Orbis
International students are warmly welcomed at The Orbis Schools. Our structured CBSE curriculum, English-medium instruction and strong pastoral care make the transition to education in India smooth and rewarding.

## Support for New Families
Our admission team assists with documentation, school visits and settling in. For international students, we provide additional orientation and language support to ensure every child thrives from day one.
