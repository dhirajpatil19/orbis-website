## International Students
International Students Due to increased globalisation, the many overseas employment opportunities availed of by Indians now returning home, and expatriates working in India, such parents are seeking schools best suited for their wards' education, emulating the best international school standards.
The Orbis Schools, Pune, are ideal education destinations for international students. They offer a nurturing environment that follows international education standards yet is grounded in the proven intrinsic Indian ways of education. This allows your child to seamlessly assimilate as they navigate the initial days at the new school. Our team of teachers discreetly watches over the new students as they tentatively make themselves comfortable in a new environment and seek new friends and mentors. As they take these ginger steps, the Orbis Schools provide a conducive environment to make them feel at home.
At The Orbis School, we welcome international students from diverse backgrounds and cultures, providing them with a supportive and inclusive learning environment. Our commitment to excellence in education makes us one of India's best schools for international students. We strive to create an enriching academic experience that prepares students for success in a globalised world.
International students seeking admission to The Orbis School must submit certain mandatory documents, including a copy of their passport, a copy of the No Objection Certificate (NOC), and all other class mandates. Completed forms must be submitted to our Admission Processing Cell along with the necessary documents for admission to our international school.
For further information about international student admissions, please contact our admissions team. We are dedicated to assisting prospective students and their families throughout admissions.
Whether you're seeking admission to the 11th class in Pune or a nurturing environment for your child for any other class, The Orbis School welcomes you to join our community of Orbis international students.
We believe in providing all children equal opportunities without discrimination based on caste, creed, gender, colour, or religion.
