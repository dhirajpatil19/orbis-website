## FAQs
## What are the fees for CBSE schools in Pune?
Fees vary; checking directly with the school for the current fee structure is best.
## What is the mode of payment for fees?
Fees can usually be paid via Payment Gateway and eNACH.
## Are the fees refundable if admission is cancelled?
Fee refund policies vary; please check the school’s fee refund policy.
## Do fees include lunch and snacks?
Lunch and snacks are not included in the tuition fees.
## Is there an option to pay the fees in instalments?
Yes, the school offers instalment payment options.
## Are there any additional fees for extracurricular activities?
There is no additional fee for extracurricular activities. However, certain electives may be charged separately.
## Do the fees include books and uniforms?
No, books and uniforms are not included in the fees.
