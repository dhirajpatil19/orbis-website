## Admission Enquiry
Looking to join our CBSE schools in Pune? Fill out our Admission Enquiry form to get more information about our academics, admission process, etc. Provide your details and specific inquiries, and our school admission team will assist you with all the details you need to make an informed decision required for your child's educational journey. Start your journey with The Orbis School today!
Welcome to The Orbis School's admission process for the academic year  2027-28! We're thrilled you're considering us for your child's educational journey.
Our streamlined admission process ensures a hassle-free experience for parents and students alike. Begin your journey by following the following steps:
Fill the Admission Enquiry form . This step allows us to understand your requirements and provide you with the necessary information about our CBSE school in Pune.
After submitting the Enquiry form , our Admission Counsellor shall contact you to facilitate a campus visit and counselling session. The Admission Counsellor shall introduce you to the Transport department to understand the tranportation rules, routes and pick up points.
After meeting the counsellor you'll receive an email containing the Registration Form.
Fill it out, attach all required documents, and proceed to pay the Registration Fee.
The Admission Counsellor shall facilitate an interaction with the Academic Co-ordinator to have a better understanding of the pedagogy and the students’ strengths and areas of improvements.
Shortlisted candidates shall receive an email providing the link to make payments. Once the parent has made all payments and submit any pending documents.
Child to attend school either when the new term begins or within three days for mid-term admissions, as the case may be. Our welcoming environment and dedicated faculty ensure a smooth transition for all students.
At The Orbis School, we prioritise academic excellence and holistic development. Our CBSE curriculum and state-of-the-art facilities ensure a well-rounded education for every student.
For admission to the academic year 2027-28, trust The Orbis Schools—a leading choice for school admission in Pune. Join us in shaping a brighter future for your child!
Please contact our admission team if you have any further questions or require assistance. We're here to help you every step of the way.
