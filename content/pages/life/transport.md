## Safe Journeys, Every Day
Our transport department runs a fleet of GPS-tracked, staff-supervised buses covering routes across Pune. Route planning is coordinated with admissions so that every family has a practical option.

## Safety First
Trained attendants on every bus, speed governance, regular vehicle audits and live tracking give parents complete peace of mind.
