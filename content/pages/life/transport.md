## Transport
Transport With more and more homes being double-income families, we understand the need for a safe, convenient transport facility is paramount as parents find it difficult to take the onus of the daily chore of dropping and picking up their children at school. Additionally, the road and parking conditions further exasperate the situation. Hence, parents are looking for a safe, convenient, and reliable facility, which is the hallmark of a good school.
The Orbis Schools’ transport facility, which began in 2010, is rated among the best in the school industry. Today, it boasts a robust young fleet of seventy-plus buses of all configurations and variations and an enviable track record.
Catering to 2200 students daily, five days a week, the transport department works clockwise on 100+ routes, ensuring all routes operate on time. Safety is the most important factor, and all the buses in the fleet are equipped with the latest RTA-compliant GPS systems, fire extinguishers, first aid, speed governors, and CCTV cameras. Our fully compliant buses are inspected annually by the authorities and reviewed by the transport committee twice a year. Each bus has a lady attendant trained and sensitised to ensure the students' wellbeing and safety.

## FAQs
## Do The Orbis Schools provide transportation?
Yes, school transportation is available for students of all classes.
## How can I track the school bus?
We provide a GPS tracking system for school buses. The parents may access the live status via the app.
## Is there an attendant on the bus?
Yes, a trained lady attendant duly sensitised to the needs, safety, security, and well-being of the students is present on every bus.
## Can I change the bus route or stop during the academic year?
We understand that circumstances may necessitate requesting a change of route or stop. We would be pleased to facilitate the request. Approvals to the formal request are subject to feasibility.
## What is the safety protocol followed on school buses?
The Orbis Schools pride themselves on their very strict adherence to safety protocols, including CCTV, GPS tracking, regular vehicle maintenance, safety drills, and audits by vigilance committees. Saturday briefing and recap sessions for the drivers and lady attendants ensure that the staff remain aware and sensitised.
All our buses have a trained and skilled lady attendant on board. Additionally, on nearly all routes, teachers use the bus facilities. Such teachers maintain strict vigil and a log of key performance indices, which include health, hygiene, and safety. All of these make the Orbis transportation services safe.
## Is the transport compliant with authorities?
Yes, great care is taken to ensure that the transport facility is compliant with all relevant authorities, including valid and subsistence permits, insurances, PUCs, CCTVs, etc.
## Do they have CCTV cameras?
Yes, two CCTV cameras are installed in each transport vehicle.
