## Progress and Promotion
Progress and Promotion The school’s academic year is divided into the two terms. The evaluation process measures and assesses a wide range of faculties to provide a complete picture of a student’s abilities and progress. Feedback is constructive, objective and sensitive respecting the uniqueness of every individual. Promotion policies are in alignment with the prescribed norms.
