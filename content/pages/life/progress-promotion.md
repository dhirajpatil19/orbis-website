## Continuous, Caring Assessment
Progress at Orbis is measured continuously — not just by exams. Observations, projects, class participation and formal assessments together paint an honest picture of each child's growth.

## Promotion With Support
Promotion decisions follow CBSE norms and are always made in the child's best interest, with parent-teacher conversations and support plans where needed.
