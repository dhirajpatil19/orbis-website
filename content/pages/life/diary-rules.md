## The Diary: School ↔ Home
The student diary is the daily bridge between school and home. It carries notices, homework, achievements and messages — and parents are encouraged to check and sign it regularly.

## Keeping It Useful
Students are expected to keep the diary neat, updated and with them daily. Teachers and parents use it for two-way notes, ensuring nothing important gets lost between campus and home.
