## Discipline as a Value, Not a Punishment
At Orbis, discipline is taught through example, clear expectations and consistent routines. We aim for self-discipline — children who understand why, not just what.
## A Respectful Community
Our code of conduct is simple and shared: respect yourself, respect others, respect the environment. Staff model it, assemblies reinforce it, and parents partner in it.
