## FAQs
FAQS
## How many terms does our CBSE school have?
The school follows a two-term academic year.
## What curriculum does the school follow?
The school follows the National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT) curriculum.

## What measures are taken to ensure hygiene in The Orbis Schools?
Regular cleaning and sanitisation are carried out. Deep cleaning is done every term. Hygiene awareness programs and audits are conducted to maintain the highest standards.
## What are the security standards at The Orbis Schools?
The security of the students is of paramount importance. The school’s safety committee monitors safety standards, and the vigilance committee does regular rounds of the school, taking special care to visit the less frequented spaces. Various other committees function as per the school’s safety and security policy. The School has designated in-charges for CCTV surveillance, GPS tracking, security personnel, and regular safety drills.
## How often are Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs) held in The Orbis Schools?
PTMs are usually held twice every term.
## When can parents interact with teachers outside of PTMs in the Orbis CBSE school?
Parents can schedule meetings with teachers by appointment on any working Saturday.
## How many students are there per division in The Orbis Schools?
The number of students per division in Pre Primary is 30, and class 1 upwards is 40.
## Are there any scholarships or financial aid options available at the school?
Scholarship and financial aid options are available; please contact the school admissions counsellors.
## What is the evaluation system in The Orbis Schools?
The school follows the CBSE evaluation system, including formative and summative assessments.
## Is Marathi compulsory in Pune CBSE schools?
Yes, Marathi is compulsory up to a certain grade as per state regulations. For a detailed explanation, please contact our admission counsellor.
