## Leading by Learning
Student councils, house systems and class leadership roles give students real responsibility from an early age — organising events, representing peers and driving school initiatives.

## The Responsible Leadership Ethos
At Orbis, leadership is taught as service. Our students learn to listen, delegate, decide and take accountability — the quiet skills of responsible leadership.
