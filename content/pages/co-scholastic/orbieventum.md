## OrbiEventum
OrbiEventum The Orbis Schools’ "Celebrate Learning!" guides their pursuit of holistic growth. In order to fulfil this aim, the school has developed special yearly events that go outside the classroom to improve students' abilities in various subjects, including sciences, athletics, literature, speech, and theatre. The literary event OrbiLoqui, the annual athletic meet OrbiSports, the interscholastic football competition OrbiCup, the thematic cultural events OrbiTheatrum and OrbiTria, the logic and science and art-focused OrbiSion, and the charitable event OrbiRun are all under the umbrella of OrbiEventum. Every student is given the chance to participate and hone their talents thanks to these events. Within the purview of OrbiEventum, the gatherings OrbiRecretio and OrbiMusica serve to promote a sense of community.
## OrbiTria
Is the Annual Speech Day followed by the Prize Distribution where all the students are awarded and acknowledged for their exceptional contributions in various fields. This evening is a culmination of inter-house activities followed by the award for the best house: the Orb of Fame.
## OrbiTheatrum
It is the most awaited cultural extravaganza of the school, participated in by all students. It is the grand stage for the children’s talents through the forms of dance, drama and singing.
## OrbiLoqui
It is the Annual Literary Event, involving all the students of the school. Age-appropriate competitions like Recitation, Spell Bee, Story-telling, Show & Tell, Spin a Yarn, Voice to the Poem, Repartee, Declamation, Debate, Extempore and Elocution are held. The event is judged by internal and external judges and hosted by the students themselves.
## OrbiSports
The Annual Athletic Meet where our budding champions display their strength and stamina. It is all about fair play, teamwork and respect for others. Students also participate in various drills and displays that demonstrate their coordination and discipline.
## OrbiSion
Is the Annual Science and Art Exhibition where our young Orbians showcase their creativity, experiments, demonstrations, models and innovations with confidence and grace. It is a great learning experience for the students as they learn hands-on.
## OrbiCup
The Inter School Football Tournament is organised by Orbis where top schools Pune participate in. At the end of this event, awards are given to the top scorer, best player, runners up and to the winning team. Values like teamwork and sportsmanship are seen on the field in great measure.
## OrbiRun
The OrbiRun is organised every year not only to celebrate fitness, but also to empower the underprivileged with donations from parents and people wanting to make a difference. The winners of each course are felicitated with medals and the others are awarded participation certificates in recognition of their journey to fitness and social empowerment.
## OrbiRecreatio
Fun outings where learning takes place outside the classrooms for staff and students.
## OrbiMusica
Is a joyous musical evening conducted every year attended by our staff members along with their families where the larger Orbis family bonds together.
