## Celebrate Learning
OrbiEventum is our annual celebration of everything Orbis — cultural shows, literary competitions, sports meets, and science and art exhibitions, all on one vibrant stage.

## Every Child Performs
Whether on the sports field, the cultural stage or the exhibition floor, every student participates and every achievement is celebrated. It is learning at its most joyful.
