## Empathy in Action
The Student Social Responsibility (SSR) programme turns compassion into practice. Students lead drives for underprivileged communities, environmental campaigns, and initiatives that make a measurable difference.

## Leaders Who Serve
Every Orbis student is encouraged to take ownership of a cause. The result: graduates who understand that leadership begins with service.
