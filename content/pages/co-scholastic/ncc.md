## NCC Empowering Youth
Our NCC unit instils discipline, patriotism and leadership through drills, camps and community service. Cadets learn teamwork, fitness and the spirit of service to the nation.

## Beyond the Parade Ground
NCC at Orbis is a leadership laboratory — cadets organise events, represent the school at rallies and camps, and carry its values into every part of school life.
