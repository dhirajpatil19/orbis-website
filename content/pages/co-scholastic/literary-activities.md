## A School of Words
From elocution to creative writing, from debating to declamation, our literary activities give every voice a stage. The annual OrbiLoqui festival celebrates the spoken and written word across grades.

## Why It Matters
Strong communication is the first skill of leadership. Our literary calendar ensures students practise it regularly, confidently and joyfully.
