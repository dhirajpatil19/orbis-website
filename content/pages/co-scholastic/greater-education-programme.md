## Education for the Whole Child
The Greater Education Programme (GEP) is our umbrella for everything that grows the child beyond the syllabus — leadership, values, service, creativity and physical well-being.

## What GEP Includes
Leadership labs, community service, public speaking, environmental stewardship and wellness education — woven into the school week, not bolted on as an afterthought.
