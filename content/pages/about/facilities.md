## Facilities
School Facilities Our schools have a modern infrastructure designed to foster a conducive learning environment. From spacious classrooms to advanced laboratories, we ensure that every aspect of our facilities enhances the learning experience.
We boast well-equipped science labs for scientific exploration where students can conduct hands-on experiments and delve into the fascinating world of Biology, Chemistry, and Physics. Step into the future with the AI and Robotics Lab, where innovation meets imagination. Our state-of-the-art facility is dedicated to pushing the boundaries of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Robotics technology, driving breakthroughs that shape the future of automation, human-machine interaction, and intelligent systems.
In line with our commitment to holistic development, we offer a range of extracurricular facilities. Our sports ground hosts various activities, including cricket, football, basketball, archery, skating, and throwball, providing ample opportunities for students to nurture their athletic talents. Moreover, our NCC training and football field promotes student discipline and teamwork. Our digital classrooms are equipped with cutting-edge technology to enhance the teaching-learning process, while our well-stocked library and reading room encourage a love for reading and research among students.

Our campuses are CCTV-covered, ensuring a safe and secure environment for all. Additionally, our sick bay is equipped to provide immediate medical attention should the need arise. A qualified nurse is at hand to attend to the needs of the students and staff.
We understand the importance of convenience for parents and students alike. That's why we offer transport facilities for hassle-free commuting. Our mission at the school transport facility is to create a safe and supportive environment for students during their daily commute. We aim to provide reliable transportation services that prioritise safety, punctuality, and comfort, allowing students to focus on their education without worrying about transportation logistics.
Focusing on providing a holistic education, The Orbis School strives to offer facilities that cater to the diverse needs of our students. From academic excellence to extracurricular enrichment, our facilities are designed to nurture every aspect of a student's growth and development. Join us at The Orbis School and experience excellence in education like never before!
