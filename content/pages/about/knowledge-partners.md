## Learning Beyond the Classroom
We believe the best education is a connected one. The Orbis Schools collaborate with knowledge partners across academia, technology and the arts to bring real-world expertise into our classrooms and our students into real-world contexts.

## How We Partner
Our partnerships take many forms: visiting faculty and workshops, olympiad and competitive-exam coaching, robotics and AI curriculum support, cultural and literary festivals, and community service initiatives.
