## Our Knowledge Partners
Knowledge Partners
## CBSE
The Orbis School, Keshav Nagar and The Orbis School, Mundhwa are affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) up to Senior Secondary School. Both the schools actively participate in various workshops conducted by CBSE, so as to keep them updated on the latest teaching-learning deliveries.
## Google Powered Workspace
The Orbis Schools are GSuite subscribers since 2013, and have maximised productivity and collaborative work by extensive use of the various Google Apps. Online workshops, training, meetings, video conferences and online classes are carried out on Meet and Google Classroom. Academic and Administrative work is smooth sailing thanks to Google Sheets, Google Docs, Google Forms, Google Slides in the Google Drive.
## Microsoft Enabled Campuses
The Orbis Schools have been Microsoft Aspire School Program (MASP) enabled campuses since 2017. Membership to this prestigious programme gives us access to Microsoft Office 365 & In Tune license for all faculty, students and labs, usage rights & access to the latest Windows Education, student training on next generation technology in gaming/ development and entrepreneurship, teacher training on 21st century teaching tools and access to Microsoft Empowered Teacher Sessions and Microsoft Student Associate Engagements.
## Teach a Man to Fish
The school has received a Silver Level Accreditation from Teach a Man to Fish, an organization in the UK, for its participation in the international level competition, School Enterprise Challenge.
## Quick Heal Seqrite Protected Campuses
In our continuous endeavour to keep our children and schools safe, The Orbis Schools have subscribed to the Quick heal Seqrite Academic package for antivirus since 2017 where every computer in the school has antivirus installed on it in addition to the Firewall installed in the school network, providing cyber security and keeping malware and undesirable sites out of school network.
## Chrysalis
Chrysalis is an interactive syllabus for the younger ones. A variety of activity-based lessons makes experiential learning a very rewarding experience.
It is a new, progressive, activity-based curriculum which focuses on thinking and speaking skills. It encourages teamwork and sensibility. This curriculum emphasizes more on application of knowledge acquired than on rote memorization.
Children understand the objectives better and come out with amazing solutions. They work together as a team, helping those with difficulties in understanding/ learning. This is the aim of our education system to build team spirit.
## Yardstick
Yardstick is an experiential learning program to develop the cognitive domain of young learners. It makes every child feel special and unique by doing the activity independently and drawing individual inferences.
## School Cinema
School Cinema is a film-based learning module combined with a fun and exciting workbook to reaffirm values, morals, attitudes and life skills in children with a perfect blend of entertainment & education. It uses short films to shape discussions and to prompt the viewer to think differently and even encourages introspection among students, teachers, and parents.
## Zoomer
Physical activity is a good way to build competence and endurance. The Zoomer activity at The Orbis School Pre-Primary wing, is loved by the children. Zoomer sport is fun, interactive and a superb fitness program. It is a structured curriculum, based on a multi-sport approach, which gives children inspiration and confidence to be physically active.
## Free Runners - Orbis Run
The Orbis School in association with Free Runners conducts the 'Orbis Run', a great opportunity to promote a healthy lifestyle and to raise funds for Infrastructural and IT requirements of an underprivileged school.
## Edunext
Edunext's School Management Software and School mobile app are opted by more than 600 top schools in India. Edunext allows The Orbis Schools stakeholders to access the complete data on a common platform where they can perform their designated tasks and operations such as admissions process, attendance, fees, term-wise examinations, results, and other records. The Management ERP automates the calculation of complex data management processes.
Edunext also has a built-in communication module where both parents and teachers can interact in real time.
## Vizuara
Vizuara is India’s first provider of a research-oriented practical AI Curriculum. The curriculum is designed by PhD graduates from MIT and Purdue University in the USA. The curriculum is based on the NEP framework and appropriately updated with concepts relevant to current industry trends. Vizuara follows a ‘No-Code AI’ pedagogy. With this innovative approach, students can actively engage with AI concepts and build projects without the need to delve into the complexities of coding. This streamlined and accessible method of learning offers a practical, hands-on experience for students, making AI a more accessible and engaging subject for all.
## Tata ClassEdge
Tata ClassEdge provides instructional e-learning curriculum and technology solutions to top schools in Robotics. Best schools use Tata ClassEdge ThinkEdge Labs to create an ecosystem to foster learning, creativity, and innovation in Coding, Artificial Intelligence.
The Orbis School has setup a Tata Class Edge English Language Lab where interactive digital content catering to listening and speaking skills is used in a stimulating lab environment. It focuses on vocabulary and grammar as well as the finer nuances of language like intonation, stress patterns, the pace of speech, and so on.
## Times NiE
The Times Newspaper in Education (Times NiE) program at The Orbis School enriches students' learning by integrating current news into education. Through the Times of India Student Edition, a newspaper tailored for young readers, students engage with relevant, up-to-date content that enriches classroom discussions and promotes active learning. This initiative fosters a lifelong reading habit, empowering students to become well-informed, confident, and globally aware citizens. With a blend of cutting-edge knowledge and interactive activities, Times NiE inspires a passion for learning and equips students to tackle modern challenges with insight and competence. The program also provides a platform for students to showcase their articles and artwork in the newspaper, encouraging creativity and self-expression.
## RIDS
The Orbis Schools' proudly partners with the British Council’s RIDS, integrating internationalism into our curriculum. Students engaged in projects on climate action, cultural exchange, sustainability, and global health, including a collaboration with GEMS United Indian School, Abu Dhabi. This association has enriched learning, fostered global citizenship, and strengthened our values of sustainability and intercultural understanding.
## Teachmint
Teachmint is an AI-powered classroom technology platform that enables smart, interactive, and future-ready learning. Used by educators and institutions in over 50 countries, it combines digital teaching tools, classroom management, assessments, and communication into a single, seamless ecosystem.
At The Orbis Schools, Teachmint enhances the learning experience through Teachmint X, a Google EDLA-certified, AI-powered digital board. It transforms traditional classrooms into engaging learning spaces where students collaborate, explore concepts visually, and actively participate in lessons. The platform also empowers teachers with innovative tools that make teaching more interactive, efficient, and impactful.
## TEDx
TEDx is a globally recognised platform inspired by TED's mission of "Ideas Worth Spreading." It encourages individuals to share innovative ideas, inspiring stories, and meaningful conversations that create a positive impact.
At The Orbis Schools, TEDx Youth provides students with an opportunity to think critically, communicate confidently, and present ideas that matter. Students actively participate in planning, organising, and hosting TEDx events while developing leadership, creativity, collaboration, and public speaking skills. The experience nurtures confident, future-ready learners who are prepared to inspire change within their communities and beyond.
## Open Door
Open Door is a life skills and well-being programme designed to support students' holistic development. Through interactive activities and meaningful discussions, it helps children build self-awareness, emotional resilience, empathy, and effective communication skills.
The programme encourages responsible decision-making, teamwork, and positive relationships while creating a safe space for self-expression. By complementing academic learning, Open Door equips students with the confidence and life skills needed to thrive both inside and outside the classroom.
## Butterfly EduFields
Butterfly EduFields is an innovative educational initiative dedicated to transforming learning from rote memorisation to meaningful, hands-on experiences. Aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP), Butterfly EduFields provides STEM-based classroom kits and experiential learning programmes for students from Grades 1 to 10, making Science and Mathematics engaging, interactive, and application-oriented.
At The Orbis Schools, Butterfly EduFields enables students to explore concepts through experiments, activities, and Do-It-Yourself (DIY) kits that complement the school curriculum. The programme helps simplify complex concepts, promotes critical thinking, encourages collaborative and independent learning, and nurtures problem-solving and innovation, empowering students to become confident, curious, and future-ready learners.
## Sunderji's Global Academia
Sunderji's Global Academia is a leading educational organisation dedicated to promoting inclusive education and empowering educators with the knowledge and skills to support diverse learning needs. Through specialised training programmes, it equips teachers with practical strategies to create inclusive, empathetic, and learner-centred classrooms where every child feels valued and supported.
At The Orbis Schools, Sunderji's Global Academia has enabled our educators to strengthen their understanding of inclusive teaching practices through comprehensive professional development. The programme focuses on recognising diverse learning needs, fostering student confidence through Universal Design for Learning (UDL), understanding behaviour with empathy, and creating classrooms where every learner can thrive. This initiative reflects our commitment to building an inclusive school environment that celebrates every child's unique potential.
