## FAQs
## Is preschool and nursery the same?
Preschool is a general term that includes playgroup, nursery, junior, and senior kindergarten (JrKG and SrKG) classes. Some people refer to a nursery as a preschool.
## What is the next stage after preschool?
The next stage after preschool, which is after Senior Kindergarten, is Lower Primary.
## What is the difference between LKG and preschool?
LKG (Lower Kindergarten) is a specific class within the preschool.
## What is the age for admission to a nursery in Pune?
Nursery age generally starts from 2.5 to 3 years.
## What are the age criteria for admission to Kindergarten in a CBSE school?
LKG typically starts at 3.5 to 4 years, and UKG at 4.5 to 5 years.
## What is the best age to start preschool in a CBSE school?
The best age is around 2.5 years.
## What is the best age to start nursery school in a CBSE school?
Nursery usually starts at around 2.5 to 3 years.
## What age group is suitable for Play Group?
Play Group is suitable for children aged 2 to 2.5 years.
## Can parents visit the school anytime to see their child?
We are pleased to have parents demonstrate their keenness to better understand the school. We welcome visits; however, a prior appointment is requested.
## How does the school ensure the safety of children?
Safety is ensured through secure premises: gated with high compound walls, monitoring of every visitor through an app, fire drills, fire fighting systems, avoidance of sharp corners, various committees, CCTV surveillance, safe transport and trained staff.
