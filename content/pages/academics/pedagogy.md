## The Orbis Way of Teaching
Our pedagogy turns work into play and questions into quests. We blend the rigour of the CBSE curriculum with inquiry-based, experiential methods that make children active owners of their learning.

## Key Principles
Concept-first learning, collaborative projects, technology-enhanced classrooms, and continuous (not just terminal) assessment. Every lesson plan is designed to ignite the young mind.
