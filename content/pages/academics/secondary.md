## Life at Secondary
Secondary School in Pune for Class 9th and 10th At The Orbis Schools, our teaching pedagogy is built upon three pillars: Listening, Doing, and Interacting. Students learn best when actively engaging with the material, so our approach emphasises hands-on learning and interactive experiences. By encouraging students to create original work, we empower them to reach the highest levels of thinking and creativity.
In line with our commitment to providing a modern and forward-thinking education, we expose our students to the latest technology, including artificial intelligence and coding. This equips them with essential skills for the digital age and prepares them for the challenges of tomorrow.
Additionally, we have partnered with a UK-based organisation Teach a Man to Fish to offer our students opportunities to develop business and life skills through real youth-led businesses. These experiences enhance their academic learning and teach them valuable leadership, accounting, and salesmanship skills, making them employable in today's competitive job market.
Furthermore, we believe in instilling a sense of social responsibility in our students. Through initiatives like our interact clubs, students aged 12-18 participate in humanitarian service activities such as blood donation camps and plastic and e-waste collection drives. Our diverse range of (CCA) clubs, including music, dance, needlework, scouts & guides, and archery, provide students with opportunities for non-academic enrichment and personality development.
At our school, summer buzzes with excitement as students dive into enriching internships and participate in the dynamic elections of the student council, culminating in the grand investiture ceremony. We proudly collaborate with European schools through Europe meets India (Eumind) activities, fostering global connections. Founders’ Day celebrates our rich heritage, while the International Award for Young  People (I.A.Y.P) projects ignite the spirit of adventure and service.
Our annual literary event, Orbiloqui (Literary Event), and drama spectacle, Orbitria, showcase our students' creativity and talent. Orbisports brings thrilling athletic competitions. Field trips offer invaluable learning experiences beyond the classroom. Join us in celebrating a season of growth, achievement, and unforgettable memories!
To ensure the holistic development of our students, we conduct regular life skills sessions focusing on healthy social-emotional development. Through interactions with experts from various fields, students gain valuable insights into the key skills and qualities needed to become responsible and well-rounded individuals.
As a top secondary school in Pune, we offer admission for classes 9th and 10th, providing a nurturing environment where students can thrive academically, socially, and emotionally.
*For promotion to the next grade, students are required to obtain at least 40% marks in every subject individually.
