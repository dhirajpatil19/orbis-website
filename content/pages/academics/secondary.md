## Rigour with Mentorship
In Classes 9–10, students encounter the full rigour of the CBSE curriculum. Our teachers provide structured mentoring, regular assessments and personal attention so that board preparation never feels like a lonely climb.

## Balanced Excellence
Sports, NCC, co-scholastic activities and wellness programmes continue alongside academics — because a healthy, happy student performs best.
