## Life at Upper Primary
Upper Primary School in Pune for Class 6th to 8th At The Orbis Schools, we are dedicated to nurturing students with 21st-century skills and preparing them to face life's challenges with confidence and resilience.
We understand that education goes beyond textbooks, so we believe in providing practical learning experiences for our students. Our school teaches students through paired activities conducted using Science and Mathematics kits. These hands-on experiences enhance their understanding of concepts and foster critical thinking and problem-solving skills.
Incorporating robotics sessions into our curriculum is a testament to our commitment to adapting to the changing needs of the modern world. These sessions introduce students to the exciting field of Artificial Intelligence and promote STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts and Mathematics) learning, ensuring that our students are well-prepared for the future.
But education isn't just about academic knowledge—it's also about imparting essential life skills. That's why we offer unique initiatives like School Cinema, where students in classes 5 and 6 learn important life lessons through movies and film pedagogy. Through this innovative approach, we address topics related to Social Emotional Learning, Life Skills, Mental Well-being, and Social Skills, empowering our students to navigate the complexities of life with resilience and empathy.
Every child's overall growth is encouraged at The Orbis School through skill-building extracurricular activities.  While our assessments increase their understanding and gives them the self-assurance to respond, public speaking activities like declamation help them become more confident and articulate and broaden their vocabulary. Cooperation, mental toughness, and endurance development are all facilitated by sports and games. Students are provided with ample opportunities to engage with the environment and make significant contributions. Development of skills is essential for this age range. Education is provided in schools on everything from financial literacy to coding. Projects and tasks assigned in this section of the school stimulate their imagination and brain development.
As a leading institution for upper primary education, we strive to provide a holistic learning experience that equips students with the skills they need to succeed in the 21st century. Whether you're seeking admission for upper primary classes: 6th class admission, 7th class admission, or 8th class admission, The Orbis Schools is your partner in shaping a brighter future for your child.
*For promotion to the next grade, students are required to obtain at least 40% marks in every subject individually.
