## Deepening the Thinker
Classes 6–8 are the years of intellectual awakening. Students move from learning to read to reading to learn, engaging with abstract concepts in Science and Math, and taking on independent projects.

## Confidence in Every Arena
Debates, the Robotics Lab, inter-school competitions and leadership roles in house activities prepare students for the demands of secondary school.
