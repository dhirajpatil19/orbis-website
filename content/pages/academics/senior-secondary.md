## Life at Senior Secondary
Senior Secondary School in Pune for Class 11th - Class 12th The last two years of 11th and 12th grade are pivotal for students transitioning from a broad spectrum of subjects in class 10 to choosing specific streams for their future endeavours. Recognising the significance of these crucial years, The Orbis School, esteemed as one of the best schools in India, is committed to guiding students through this transitional phase and shaping their career paths.
At The Orbis School, we understand the importance of assisting students in making informed decisions regarding their academic and career pursuits. To facilitate this, we organise University fairs, Career sessions with esteemed universities, and Counseling sessions to help students explore their interests and aspirations. Our experienced teachers provide year-round guidance to prepare students for board examinations, university admissions, and competitive entrance exams.
We offer various subjects to cater to diverse interests and career aspirations, including Physical Education, Economics, Entrepreneurship, Political Science, Psychology, Informatics Practices, Mass Media, and Computer Science, alongside the traditional Science and Commerce subjects.
In addition to academic excellence, we prioritise providing our students with international exposure. The school actively participates in Exchange programs and foreign awards projects such as EUMIND and IAYP, offering students unparalleled opportunities for growth and development. These initiatives broaden their horizons and contribute to their overall personal and academic development.
At our school, the senior secondary section is filled with excitement and opportunities for personal growth. Students engage in enriching internships and participate in the dynamic elections of the student council, leading to the grand investiture ceremony. Our collaboration with European schools through Eumind activities, fosters global connections.
Founders’ Day is a celebration of a different kind, while we acknowledge our rich heritage we reach out to our social venture partners and support their outreach programs to cancer patients,  environment protection, child safety, the blind etc.
The spirit of adventure and service is ignited by our projects and activities. Our yearly drama production, Orbitria, and literary event, Orbiloqui, highlight the inventiveness and talent of our students. Exciting sporting events are provided by OrbiSports, and field trips provide priceless opportunities for learning outside of the classroom. This phase is characterised by development, success, and life-long memories!
As a leading CBSE senior secondary school in Pune, The Orbis School offers admission to 11th grade, providing a conducive environment where students can excel academically and prepare for their future endeavours. With our focus on holistic education and comprehensive support systems, including Orbis Sr. Secondary, we aim to nurture the potential of every student and prepare them for success in their higher education and beyond.
*For promotion to the next grade, students are required to obtain at least 40% marks in every subject individually.

## FAQs
## What streams are available in Senior Secondary?
The available streams in Senior Secondary are Science, Commerce, and Humanities.
## Why is senior secondary education important at a regular school?
Senior secondary education is important as it prepares students for higher education and future career opportunities. It provides students with opportunities to continue their holistic education, all within the confines of a safe environment.
## What documents are required for admission?
Required documents for admission include class 8th, 9th, and 10th marksheets, a transfer certificate, and identification documents.
## How do I apply for admission to Senior Secondary?
You can apply for admission online, followed by an interview process.
## How are advanced learners supported?
Advanced learners are supported through enrichment programs, Olympiad training, and advanced coursework.
## Is CBSE good for Class 11?
Yes, the CBSE curriculum is available and suitable for Class 11 students as it follows the competitive exam framework.
## Does CBSE have 11th and 12th?
Yes, CBSE offers education for both 11th and 12th grades.
## What is the new education policy 2024 for 11th and 12th classes?
The new education policy 2024 emphasises multidisciplinary studies, skill development, and flexibility in subject choices for students in classes 11 and 12.
