## Life at Lower Primary
Lower Primary School in Pune for Class 1st To 5th At The Orbis Schools, we nurture young minds through experiential learning right from the early stages of their education journey. Our classrooms are not just rooms; they are think rooms where students engage in hands-on activities and inquiry-based learning. We proudly implement the Chrysalis curriculum, which is designed to unlock every child's extraordinary potential.
Our curriculum emphasises experiential learning, focusing on enquiry-based and hands-on approaches through science kits. This activity-based learning is carefully mapped to the syllabus, enabling children to understand, appreciate and apply the concepts taught.
We offer an age-wise structured physical activity curriculum supported by international-grade equipment and tools. Regular bi-annual assessments ensure that students' progress is tracked effectively, allowing us to tailor our approach to their needs.
At The Orbis Schools, we are committed to catering to every child's needs and providing them with a nurturing environment in which they can thrive. Unsurprisingly, we are recognised as one of the top 10 schools in Pune.
At our schools, we foster a well-rounded education through a variety of activities that engage students beyond the traditional classroom experience. These activities not only enhance learning but also promote creativity, teamwork, and personal growth. Our students take part in co curricular clubs, exhibitions, inter-house and inter-school competitions, and numerous literary, cultural, and athletic activities hosted by the school. In addition to improving their educational experience, these exposures help them develop lifelong friendships and abilities that will benefit them even after they pass out from school.
Join us for Lower Primary Education, where your child's journey begins with hands-on learning experiences and personalised attention. Whether you're seeking admission to the Lower Primary class or seeking school admission for 1st standard or 5th class, The Orbis Schools is the place to be.
*For promotion to the next grade, students are required to obtain at least 40% marks in every subject individually.
